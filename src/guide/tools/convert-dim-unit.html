Settings

Convert Dim Unit (m to ft)

VIDDIMMTOFT
vidDimMToFt.lsp

What it does

Changes the text of selected aligned or linear dimensions from metres to feet and inches, e.g. 3.92 becomes 12' 10".

Setup

  • Drawing units must be metres.
  • Works on aligned, rotated and linear dimensions only.

How to use

  1. Click Settings > Convert Dal Unit: Convert (or type VIDDIMMTOFT).
  2. Choose an option (Enter = Override): Override shows feet only, Prefix shows feet on top and metres below, Suffix shows metres on top and feet below, Reset goes back to the measured value.
  3. Select the dimensions. The command reports how many were updated.

Notes

  • Whole numbers only. Inches are always two digits and rounded, and 12 inches carries into feet.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Instant ft/in annotation for drawings that must show imperial units.
  • Prefix / Suffix modes keep both units on the dimension.
  • Reset restores the true measured value at any time.

Cons

  • Whole inches only - no fractional inches.
  • It is a text override: edited dimensions do not update when the geometry changes.
  • Only aligned, rotated and linear dimensions - no radial or angular.
